Snag.Ai — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Snag.Ai ("the App") is a QA/QC site-inspection app for construction projects. This page explains what data the App collects, how it's used, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, job title, and company name, provided by you when you register.
  • Inspection data: the projects, reports, observations, and photos you create while using the App, along with your saved signature image.
  • Device-local biometric opt-in: if you enable app-lock, the App only stores a flag that this device is enrolled — actual fingerprint/face verification is performed entirely by your device's operating system. No biometric data ever leaves your device or reaches our servers.

How we use this information

Data you create is used solely to provide the App's core functionality: storing and syncing your inspection reports, generating PDF exports, and (optionally) using photos you explicitly submit for AI-assisted analysis to suggest a report's title, description, and severity.

Where data is stored

Reports and photos are stored locally on your device first, so the App works fully offline. When you have connectivity, this data syncs to our server (hosted on Google Firebase/Firestore) so it's backed up and accessible across your sync history.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase/Firestore — stores your account and report data.
  • Google Gemini API — only when you tap "AI photo analysis," the selected photo is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a suggested description. No other data is sent.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share your personal information with third parties except the service providers listed above, which are necessary to operate the App.

Data retention and deletion

You can delete a registered project from within the App (Project Settings). To request deletion of your account and all associated data, contact us at the email below.

Children's privacy

The App is intended for professional construction-industry use and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
